## 🔍 How it works

The software acts as a partner for your notes. Here is the process it follows:

1. Scanning: The program reads the text files in your selected folder.
2. Understanding: It identifies key topics and themes in your writing.
3. Linking: It creates pathways between files that share similar content.
4. Structuring: It builds a table of contents to help you find information.

This process keeps your personal or team wiki growing as you add more files. You do not need to manually categorize items. The system handles the heavy lifting so your library stays tidy.

## 📋 Common questions

### Does my information stay private?
Yes. The software runs on your computer. Your files live on your local hard drive. Your data does not travel to external servers.

### Can I choose which files to include?
Yes. You choose the specific folder that wikix monitors. You can move files in or out of this folder at any time.

### Does the software require a subscription?
No. wikix works as a stand-alone tool. You download it once and use it.

### What if I delete a file?
If you delete or move a file, the software updates the links the next time you open the app. It keeps your index accurate.

## ⚖️ Managing your library

You should keep your source files in plain text format. Plain text ensures the software can read your content without issues. If you use images or charts, place them in a sub-folder within your main wiki directory. wikix tracks these assets automatically.

## ⚙️ Maintenance tips

Every few weeks, open the application to refresh your knowledge graph. This process ensures that new notes link correctly to older ones. If the software seems slow, ensure you have enough free space on your drive. Always close the program before you move your main wiki folder. This habit prevents errors in the database.

## 🌐 Expanding your reach

You can share your wiki with colleagues by syncing your folder to a shared drive.
